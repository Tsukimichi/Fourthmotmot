To My Beloved Shayne,

If ever the heavens asked what love could be, I would speak thy name before all constellations. Thou art the only flower within my garden, the only bird within my sky, the only fish within my sea, and the only star within my firmament.

I named the moon after thee, not because it was beautiful, but because it was the only thing that reminded me that even from afar we still share the same light.

My love for thee is like the deep sea — vast beyond measure, steadfast beneath every storm, and faithful even where no eye can see.

Should distance stretch across kingdoms and time ask us to wait through countless seasons, my heart shall remain where it hath always chosen to dwell: with thee.

For if I spoke of love, I would tell them of us. And if I spoke of forever, I would simply say Shayne.
